TABLE — SCENARIO SCALING RATIOS: B_MED
==============================================================================
Higher organic load growth (industrial + electrification), EIA-reference generation

Scaling ratios applied to each fuel type and load in each ERCOT weather
zone of the 2000-bus synthetic Texas system, for planning years 2030, 2035, 2040, 2045.

HOW TO READ:
  Each value is the FINAL ratio relative to the 2022 baseline, i.e.
      future_capacity = ratio x 2022_capacity
  1.00 = unchanged from 2022   2.00 = doubled   0.80 = 20% retired
  Values are applied per bus according to that bus's zone_id.
  Rows marked * carry a zone-specific value; unmarked rows inherit the
  scenario default shown in the first row of each block.

Load ratios are derived from ERCOT long-term load forecasts; generation
ratios from EIA Annual Energy Outlook projections adjusted per scenario.
Data-center demand is NOT included here — it is modeled separately as
nodal load in the corresponding _dc scenario variants.
